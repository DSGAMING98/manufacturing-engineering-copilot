# DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: Example discrepancy handling

This fictional internal example is for software testing only. It is NOT an approved shop-floor procedure, published standard, or authorization to disposition material.

If a dimensional measurement falls outside its specified limits, flag the discrepancy, preserve the original measurement record, and notify the authorized quality reviewer. Do not silently alter the observation or declare the product acceptable.

A suspected measurement error may justify rechecking the measurement method, gauge condition, unit, and part identification under an authorized local procedure. A recheck does not automatically cancel the original discrepancy.

Only appropriately authorized personnel may decide whether additional inspection, rework, segregation, or another disposition is allowed. This demonstration does not define real facility release criteria.
