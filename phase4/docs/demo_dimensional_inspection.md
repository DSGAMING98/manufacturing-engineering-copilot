# DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: Dimensional inspection procedure

This is a fictional internal training example written for this software demonstration. It is NOT a published standard, authorized industrial instruction, or production acceptance rule.

For a single dimension with a nominal value and a symmetric plus/minus tolerance, calculate the lower limit as nominal minus tolerance and the upper limit as nominal plus tolerance. An observed value within the inclusive interval meets this one dimensional criterion only.

Before recording an inspection result, confirm the measurement unit, drawing revision, feature identity, equipment identification, and the inspection record. If any required information is missing, request clarification rather than guessing.

Example: nominal shaft diameter 20.00 mm with tolerance plus/minus 0.05 mm gives inclusive limits from 19.95 mm to 20.05 mm. A measured diameter of 20.03 mm meets this single dimensional criterion; a measured diameter of 20.09 mm does not.

Passing one dimensional measurement does not establish overall product conformity. Other applicable requirements and authorized review may still be necessary.
