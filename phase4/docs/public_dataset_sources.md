# Public observed manufacturing datasets

## UCI Steel Plates Faults

The Steel Plates Faults dataset contains 1,941 observed records described by 27 geometric and luminosity measurements. Each record is assigned to one of seven surface-fault classes: Pastry, Z_Scratch, K_Scratch, Stains, Dirtiness, Bumps, or Other_Faults.

The dataset was donated by M. Buscema, S. Terzi, and W. Tastle. Its UCI dataset DOI is 10.24432/C5J88N and its license is Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

## UCI SECOM

The SECOM dataset contains 1,567 records from a semiconductor manufacturing process. It provides 590 anonymized sensor and process measurements together with a pass-or-fail label. The published data contain missing measurements and 104 failure-labelled records.

The dataset was donated by Michael McCann and Adrian Johnston. Its UCI dataset DOI is 10.24432/C54305 and its license is Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

## Scope

These public datasets support bounded classification and risk-screening experiments. They are not operating procedures, standards, machine-control instructions, or evidence of performance at a different manufacturing plant.
