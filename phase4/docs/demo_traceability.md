This is a fictional training note, not an independently verified industrial policy or an external standard.

An inspection record can identify the relevant feature, stated nominal dimension, tolerance, actual measurement, unit, drawing identifier, revision, time, and measuring equipment. Record completeness must be assessed against the actual organization's approved procedures.

When the applicable drawing or procedure revision cannot be confirmed, request an authorized source document before claiming compliance. Do not fabricate ISO, ASME, or other external standard citations.

A retrieved document excerpt supports only the specific statement shown. It cannot establish that a manufacturing organization has approved the procedure or that a particular part is released for use.
